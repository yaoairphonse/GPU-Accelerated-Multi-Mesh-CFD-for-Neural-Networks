CRM wall-pressure normal-probe experiment

Final disposition: rejected as the primary CRM accuracy correction; production default remains unchanged.

Decision. Cancel further tuning and production adoption of the pressure-only, fixed wall-normal probe as a remedy for the CRM load error. It is numerically safe, but it did not materially improve surface pressure, lift, or pitching moment. The next numerical work should target the SA face operator and its DP-LUR coupling.

Which proposal was evaluated?

The motivating proposal was an optional, wall-function-like correction for the first cut cell: use the true solid-surface normal to sample pressure or other wall quantities farther into the fluid, correct the surface layer, and feed the correction back into the flow calculation. The concern was that a Cartesian cut-cell centroid can be a poor pressure sample, especially where pressure drag and pitching moment are sensitive to local shocks and curved geometry.

A deliberately narrow first stage was implemented to avoid coupling an unverified wall model to density, temperature, velocity, viscous stress, and Spalart–Allmaras. Each exact wall fragment received two fixed fluid-side donors near 1.5h and 2.5h. Their one-sided pressure extrapolation was guarded for positivity and range, then blended 25% with the original centroid pressure. The same corrected pressure was used in the wall residual, surface output, and integrated loads. Conservative control states were not overwritten.

The rejected item is specifically this pressure-only normal-probe branch as an accuracy path. This experiment does not prove that every EOS-consistent wall state reconstruction or calibrated wall model is invalid; those broader ideas were not implemented, and are not the next priority because of their stronger interaction with turbulence closure.

Why the proposal was rejected

  1. The correction was active but too small. It changed local Cp, including a maximum pointwise difference of 0.269, but its area-weighted effect was negligible.
  2. The global error did not respond. CL and CM remained wrong by roughly 31% and 45%, so centroid pressure sampling is not the dominant error source.
  3. The transient mode was unchanged. Both branches followed the same low-frequency load trajectory. The final global total residual was about 0.00371 and the near-field total residual about 0.1256.
  4. More blending would add risk without evidence. A stronger wall correction would increasingly alter the flow and interact with SA without a measured trend toward the reference solution.

These endpoints are not converged aerodynamic predictions. Their value is the controlled A/B result: under identical transient conditions, the proposed pressure-only correction failed to move the solution in a useful direction.

Replacement development direction

Diagnose and improve the SA convective/diffusive face discretization and the associated DP-LUR implicit action. Validate that change from the same checkpoint using CPU/CUDA agreement, conservation, a time-matched load window, and surface Cp—not a selected endpoint. Wall-normal or prism-layer mesh generation remains outside this path.
